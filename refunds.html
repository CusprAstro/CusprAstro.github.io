Refunds and cancellations

Manage membership through Gumroad.

Supporter and Pro are billed by Gumroad at $4.99/month and $9.99/month respectively once their storefronts are configured.

Cancel

Use the manage-membership link in your Gumroad receipt or Gumroad Library. Cancellation and access timing are controlled by Gumroad and the seller's configured policy. Cuspr removes paid access when Gumroad reports the membership cancelled or ended.

Refunds and chargebacks

Request a refund through the seller contact shown on the Gumroad product/receipt. If Gumroad reports a refund or chargeback, the paid entitlement is removed on revalidation.

Restore

Open Cuspr → Plans → Restore / revalidate purchase, or paste the original Gumroad license key again. A product ID is not a license key.

The owner must finalize the storefront refund language before public membership sales.
