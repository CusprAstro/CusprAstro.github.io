Membership help

Activate, restore or cancel.

Activate

After buying the Supporter or Pro membership on Gumroad, copy the license key from your receipt. In Cuspr, open Plans, select the matching edition, paste the key, and choose Verify and unlock.

Restore

On a new device, repeat activation with the same key. On the current device, use Restore / revalidate purchase. Verification checks the correct product ID and rejects cancelled, ended, refunded and chargebacked memberships.

Offline policy

Cuspr revalidates after seven days. If Gumroad is temporarily unreachable, the last active membership remains usable until 14 days after the last successful verification. After that grace date, Cuspr returns to Free until it can verify again.

Cancel

Use Gumroad's manage-membership link in the receipt or Gumroad Library. Cuspr cannot cancel billing locally.
