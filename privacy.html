Cuspr

Privacy

Local first, plainly explained.

Birth profiles, planner cache and entitlement records are stored in your browser or desktop app. Cuspr does not operate an account database and does not upload birth profiles.

Network requests

  • Place search sends the typed city name to Open-Meteo.
  • News retrieves publisher feeds through the documented feed service or Electron proxy.
  • Membership activation sends the selected Gumroad product ID and the buyer's license key to Gumroad's official verification endpoint.
  • GitHub Discussions chat uses giscus/GitHub when configured.

Membership keys

Keys are stored locally for offline access, are never intentionally logged, and can be removed in Plans. Active memberships revalidate every seven days. Offline access lasts through a 14-day grace period from the last successful verification.

Ads and affiliates

Free with Ads uses only static disclosed affiliate links. Cuspr embeds no third-party advertising script, tracking pixel, popup or automatic download.
